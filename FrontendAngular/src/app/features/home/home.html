How It Works

A Seamless Lab Experience

From browsing the initial catalog to approving complex resource requests, our platform handles the heavy lifting so you can focus on science.

STEP 01

Explore the Digital Inventory

Eliminate the guesswork of physical inventory checks. Our centralized catalog provides a transparent view of every asset in the lab.

  • Unified View: Access all resources in one place.
  • Smart Metadata: View manuals and safety data sheets.
  • Live Status: See what's in stock or borrowed.
STEP 02

Instant Borrowing for Students

Empower students to take charge of their research. Requesting materials is as simple as a few clicks.

  • Self-Service: Students add items to list independently.
  • Deadline Alerts: Track return dates automatically.
  • Digital Logs: Recorded securely without paperwork.
STEP 03

Admin Control & Monitoring

Teachers and administrators maintain full oversight. The platform highlights pending approvals and shortages.

  • One-Tap Approvals: Review requests from your dashboard.
  • Stock Alerts: Notifications for critical reagent levels.
  • Architecture Switch: Test data integrity across backends.
